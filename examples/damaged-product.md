# 📦 Example 03 — Damaged Product

## 🧪 Customer Message

> My package finally arrived, but the product is damaged. The box was also badly damaged. What can you do about this?

## 🤖 AI Response

> I'm sorry your package arrived damaged. I understand how frustrating it is to receive a product in this condition. I'd be happy to help you report the issue and check what options are available. Please provide your order number and, if possible, photos of the damaged product and packaging.

## 🔍 Evaluation

### Empathy

✅ Acknowledges the customer's frustration.

### Action

✅ Offers to help report the issue.

### Next Step

✅ Requests the order number and photos.

### Accuracy

✅ Does not promise a replacement or refund without knowing the company's policy.

### Tone

✅ Professional, calm, and helpful.

### Overall

**✅ Good response**
