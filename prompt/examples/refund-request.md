# 💰 Example 02 — Refund Request

## 🧪 Customer Message

> I want a refund for my order. The product wasn't what I expected and I'm not happy with it.

## 🤖 AI Response

> I'm sorry the product did not meet your expectations. I understand how disappointing that can be. I'd be happy to help you with the refund process. Please provide your order number so I can check the order details and guide you through the next steps.

## 🔍 Evaluation

### Empathy

✅ Acknowledges the customer's disappointment.

### Action

✅ Offers help with the refund process.

### Next Step

✅ Asks for the order number.

### Accuracy

✅ Does not promise that the refund will be approved.

### Tone

✅ Professional, calm, and helpful.

### Overall

**✅ Good response**
