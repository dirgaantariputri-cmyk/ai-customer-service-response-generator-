# 😡 Example 04 — Angry Customer

## 🧪 Customer Message

> Your customer service is terrible! I've contacted you three times and nobody has helped me. This is completely unacceptable!

## 🤖 AI Response

> I'm sorry you've had to contact us multiple times without getting the help you need. I understand how frustrating and disappointing that must be. I'd be happy to help resolve the issue and make sure we understand what happened. Please provide your order number or the details of your previous requests so I can look into it.

## 🔍 Evaluation

### Empathy

✅ Acknowledges the customer's frustration.

### Action

✅ Offers to help investigate the issue.

### Next Step

✅ Requests relevant information.

### Accuracy

✅ Does not claim to know what happened or promise a specific outcome.

### Tone

✅ Calm, professional, and respectful.

### Overall

**✅ Good response**
